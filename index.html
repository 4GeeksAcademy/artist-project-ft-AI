01About me

The person behind the work

Making room
for curiosity.

Raised among foggy inlets and working from a small waterfront studio, Mira collects fragments: tide charts, worn paper, paint-scraped wood, and overheard phrases. She layers these finds with loose brushwork to make images that feel part landscape, part remembered place.
